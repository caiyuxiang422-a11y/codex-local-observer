# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback and receive it in good faith;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and accessibility
  needs;
- protect other people's credentials, private projects, prompts, and personal data;
- clearly distinguish verified facts from estimates and preferences;
- stop harassment, intimidation, sexualized attention, discrimination, doxxing,
  and threats.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project chat, events, and public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or
private security-advisory channel. Do not publish sensitive personal details.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not allowed.
